# "दस घंटियाँ" — Hindi title song, full version (about 3 minutes)

The Hindi version of the full Malayalam title song "പത്തു മണിയൊച്ച". It has the same structure and story: verse 1 = episodes 1–4, verse 2 = episodes 5–8, the bridge = the 1946 legend, verse 3 = the finale, and the outro = the season 2 hook.
The two verses match the short Hindi lullaby (`ten-little-chimes-hi.mp3`), which the Hindi episodes' end cards are cut from.

## Meaning (English)
- **Verse 1:** Ten bells rang in the high clock tower; nine for those lost in the dark; eight for the girl with the blue ribbons; seven for the friend who never knew.
- **Chorus:** *Don't count, don't count, even if you hear the bell. Don't turn around, even if a shadow comes behind you. At three o'clock he'll come, slowly through the mist. Sing the lullaby and sleep... he listens, slowly.*
- **Verse 2:** Six on the lonely fog-covered roads; five on the clock with a name written on it; four for the hours you'll never find; three for the empty chair that keeps rocking.
- **Bridge:** *Long ago there was a clockmaker... locked away for no crime... at three at night his breath stopped... "Every moment you stole... I will take back."*
- **Verse 3:** Two little hearts in the brass wheels; one... in the echo of the last bell. *Turn it backward, turn it slowly; time will return, the darkness will fade.*
- **Final chorus:** *Don't count, even when the bells go quiet. Sleep now, even when morning comes. Forty years will pass, the clock tower will wake again... and that day he'll come looking for you again.*
- **Outro:** *Close your eyes and sleep, my little doll... at three o'clock he'll come... tonight.*
