# COUNTDOWN — Series Bible

**Format:** Animated horror short series · 10 episodes × 1:30 · Vertical (9:16) or widescreen (16:9)
**Tone:** Slow-burn dread, few jump scares, every episode ends on a stinger
**Hook:** Episodes count down from **10** to **1**. Each episode has one fewer chime. When the count hits zero, the Keeper takes everyone who heard it.

---

## Logline

In a misty hill town, the clock tower stopped working 40 years ago. One night at **3:00 AM** it chimes **ten times**, and only 16-year-old **Meera** hears it. Each night it chimes one fewer time, and each night someone disappears. Nobody else remembers them. Meera has ten nights to find out who is winding the clock.

## The Rules of the Horror (keep these consistent)

1. The clock chimes only at **3:00 AM**. Each night it chimes **one fewer** time.
2. Only people who have been **"marked"** hear it. If you hear the chimes, you are marked.
3. When someone is taken, everyone forgets them except the marked.
4. The taken are trapped as **faces inside the gears** of the tower.
5. Every stopped clock near you means **the Keeper is close**.
6. The curse returns **every 40 years**. The last time was **1986**.
7. You can only stop it by winding the clock **backward**, from **inside** the tower, at 3:00.

## Setting

**Mistvale** is a small hill town of tea estates, fog, narrow roads and a single bus stop. In the old market square stands the **Varkey Clock Tower**, a black stone tower with a cracked white clock face whose hands are missing. Below it sits the shuttered **Varkey & Sons Clock Repair** shop.

## Characters

### MEERA JOSEPH (16): the protagonist
- **Look:** Thin, dark circles under her eyes, messy shoulder-length hair, an oversized maroon hoodie with the sleeves over her hands. She always wears her late father's **silver wristwatch**, which is too big for her.
- **Personality:** Quiet and observant. She is an insomniac who counts things (stairs, tiles, breaths) to calm down. She is brave without wanting to be.
- **Arc:** She starts out afraid of being "the weird one" nobody believes. She ends as the one who stops the countdown.
- **Voice:** Soft and controlled; she whispers a lot. Her catchphrase is counting under her breath.
- **AI image prompt:** `2D animated horror style, 16-year-old Indian girl, tired eyes with dark circles, messy shoulder-length black hair, oversized maroon hoodie, large silver wristwatch, muted desaturated colors, moody rim light, character turnaround sheet, front side back views, plain background`

### ARJUN NAIR (16): best friend and skeptic
- **Look:** Tall and lanky, round glasses, a school sweater vest, and a phone always in his hand. He is the only bright color on screen (a mustard-yellow jacket).
- **Personality:** A joker who wants to be a YouTuber and films everything. He uses humor to hide fear.
- **Arc:** He laughs at Meera, then hears the chimes himself, then sacrifices himself for her in Ep 2. He comes back in Ep 1.
- **Voice:** Fast, sarcastic, cracks when scared.
- **AI image prompt:** `2D animated horror style, 16-year-old Indian boy, tall lanky, round glasses, mustard yellow jacket over school sweater vest, holding smartphone, nervous grin, muted palette, character turnaround sheet`

### AMMACHI (ROSA, 72): Meera's grandmother
- **Look:** White cotton saree, thick glasses, prayer beads, a **stopped pocket watch** on a chain around her neck. She always sits in a wooden rocking chair.
- **Secret:** She heard the chimes in **1986** and is the only person who survived. Her best friend Neha did not.
- **Arc:** She is the source of the legend (Ep 7), is taken in Ep 3, leaves the clue that saves everyone, and returns in Ep 1.
- **Voice:** Slow, warm, then suddenly cold when she talks about the Keeper.
- **AI image prompt:** `2D animated horror style, elderly Indian grandmother, white cotton saree, thick round glasses, prayer beads, old brass pocket watch on chain, sitting in wooden rocking chair, oil lamp light, warm but eerie, character sheet`

### NEHA (forever 15): the ghost girl
- **Look:** A 1986 school uniform (pale blue pinafore, two braids with ribbons), grey-blue skin, eyes always in shadow. **Water drips from her hair** even indoors. She flickers like a bad VHS tape.
- **Role:** She is the first person the Keeper took in 1986. She is not evil; she is trying to warn Meera. She communicates through mirrors, fogged glass and an old lullaby.
- **Signature:** She hums **"Ten Little Chimes"** (see the music doc).
- **AI image prompt:** `2D animated horror, ghost girl age 15, 1980s Indian school uniform pale blue pinafore, two braids with ribbons, grey-blue skin, eyes hidden in shadow, wet dripping hair, VHS glitch effect, standing still, unsettling, character sheet`

### THE KEEPER (ELIAS VARKEY): the villain
- **Look:** Over 7 feet tall and very thin, in a long black tailcoat. In place of a head he has an **old cracked clock face with no hands**. His fingers are long brass keys. You hear **ticking** wherever he walks, and every clock near him stops.
- **Backstory:** In 1946 Elias Varkey, a gifted clockmaker, built the tower. The town accused him of a theft he didn't commit and locked him inside the tower, where he died at 3:00 AM. He swore: *"I will take back every hour you stole from me."*
- **Rule:** He never runs. He is always **closer** each time you look.
- **Signature move:** He tilts his clock-face head, and the hands appear, pointing at the person he will take next.
- **AI image prompt:** `2D animated horror villain, extremely tall thin figure in long black tailcoat, head is an antique cracked white clock face with no hands, long brass key-shaped fingers, standing in fog, backlit, silhouette, terrifying but elegant, character sheet`

### Minor
- **SAM (16):** The classmate who vanishes in Ep 9. You only need a face drawing plus a "clock face" version.
- **Meera's MOM:** Voice only, off-screen ("Meera, go to sleep!"). This saves animation work.

## Visual Style Guide

- **Palette:** Desaturated blue-grey fog, deep black shadows, and **one accent color per scene**. Arjun's yellow means safety and life; **sick green** means the Keeper is near.
- **Recurring motifs:** Clocks, numbers, counting, fog, the number on screen at the end of every episode.
- **Camera:** Slow push-ins, long holds, and things moving in the background that the characters don't notice.
- **Effects:** Light film grain on every shot. A VHS glitch whenever Neha appears. Clock-hand shadows sweeping across walls.
- **Episode end card:** Black screen, a single loud **chime**, then the number in white serif type (e.g. **"9"**), and the number cracks.

## Episode Structure (every 1:30 episode)

| Time | Beat |
|------|------|
| 0:00–0:08 | **Cold open:** a creepy image plus the chime count |
| 0:08–0:12 | **Title card:** "COUNTDOWN" plus the episode number |
| 0:12–1:15 | **Story:** 3–4 short scenes |
| 1:15–1:25 | **Stinger:** the scare or reveal |
| 1:25–1:30 | **End card:** the next number cracks, and a "Ten Little Chimes" hum |
