# ഒരു റിപ്ലൈ (One Reply) — title song, Malayalam, version 4 (new lyrics)

Style: starts soft and dreamy (version 1 feel), builds to a joyful sing-along (version 3 feel), key lift on the final chorus · warm male lead, soft female harmonies, group vocals at the end · acoustic guitar, piano, ukulele, claps, whistle hook, strings · ~104 BPM · ~3 min
Story: 2 AM reel → typing "Hi" a hundred times → waiting for the blue tick → her "Thanks" → joy → bittersweet acceptance (Ending A).
