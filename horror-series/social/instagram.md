## 💬 Engagement tips
- **Reply to the first 10–20 comments** within the first hour. Early replies give a Reel more reach.
- **Pin a question comment** on each post, like the promo's.
- **Story the day after each post:** a poll like "അടുത്തത് ആര്? മീര / അർജുൻ", or a quiz like "എത്ര മണിയൊച്ച ബാക്കി?".
- **Story countdown sticker:** "എപ്പിസോഡ് N · രാത്രി 9 മണി". Followers who tap it get a reminder.
- **Same title style every time** (`COUNTDOWN · എപ്പിസോഡ് N/10`), so people can find them in order.
