# AGAIN (വീണ്ടും) — episode scripts (draft)

Same anime look as ഒരു റിപ്ലൈ; same hero **Aadhi**, younger (school). ~85 s per episode, 9:16, Malayalam.
Narrator: **adult Aadhi** (same voice as One Reply — "Akhil"), looking back. Music: AGAIN title song v1 (instrumental cuts) + whistle, chenda, crowd.
Names of schools, coaches and teammates are changed. No school name, crest or place name readable on screen.
