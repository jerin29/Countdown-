# COUNTDOWN Season 2 — "നാൽപത് (FORTY)" · Scripts (Malayalam master)

15 episodes × 1:30 · **one seamless long take per episode** (no hard cuts; the camera glides through one painted world).
Every episode follows the same frame:

| Time | Block |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | **Hook:** the episode's scariest image, held for 3 seconds, sharp hit. |
| 0:03–0:08 | **Headphone warning:** screen goes black, white text "🎧 ഹെഡ്‌ഫോൺ ഉപയോഗിക്കുക", a whisper moves from the left ear to the right: *"ഹെഡ്‌ഫോൺ വെക്കൂ…"* Then the camera pulls out of the darkness into the story. |
| 0:08–1:20 | **Story:** one continuous camera move. |
| 1:20–1:25 | **Count card:** the new number cracks onto a clock face ("15", "14", …), one deep bell. |
| 1:25–1:30 | **End credits:** Story by Jerin Joseph Sebastian · Created by Claude AI & vidIQ · Thank you for watching. Last bell fades into a breath. |

**Sound rules:** long quiet build → ~1 s of near-silence → hit. Phone-speaker scares sit in 1–5 kHz (scrapes, breath, ratchets, distorted bells, violin screech). Headphone scares: whispers that travel ear to ear, ticking that circles the head, sub rumble. Signature: the old bell slowed and reversed, ending in a breath.

**Characters:** Aadi (10) · Meera (17) · Arjun · Ammachi · Neha · Aadi's mother · The Watchers (clock-faced people) · **The Clockmaker's Daughter** ("അവൾ"), face hidden until Ep 14.

## Ep 1 · 15 · നാൽപത് (Forty)
**Hook:** Aadi's bedroom wall clock: the hands spin backwards and stop at 3:00. A child's whisper: "നാൽപത്."
**Camera path:** Aadi's face asleep → drift across the room to the wall clock → through the window → over the dark city → every window lights with a glowing clock face → down into the street.
- 0:08 Aadi (10) wakes at 3:00. Ticking everywhere, from the walls, his phone, the microwave.
- 0:20 The camera glides with him to the window. Across the city, every clock tower, phone and wall clock shows **3:00**.
- 0:35 All the clocks strike together. We count with him: one… two… The chimes don't stop at ten.
- 0:55 **ആദി:** "പന്ത്രണ്ട്… ഇരുപത്… ഇത് നിൽക്കുന്നില്ല…"
- 1:05 At **forty**, silence. One breath right behind his ear (headphone left→right).
- 1:12 In the glass, behind his reflection, a girl in a 1940s frock. Her face is hidden by her hair.
- 1:15 **അവൾ (whisper):** "ഇത്തവണ പത്തല്ല, ആദി."
**Count card:** 15.

## Ep 2 · 14 · ടിക്കുന്ന കൈത്തണ്ട (The Ticking Wrist)
**Hook:** Extreme close-up: under Meera's skin, a tiny gear turns.
**Camera path:** Meera's hostel room in Kochi → down her arm → into a magnifying mirror → out into a memory of the tower.
- 0:08 Meera (17) can't sleep. In the silence: tick… tick… from her own wrist.
- 0:25 She presses her ear to it. **മീര:** "ഇത് എന്റെ ഹൃദയമല്ല."
- 0:40 A gear-shaped mark rises under the skin and rotates.
- 0:55 Her phone buzzes: an unknown number, a voice note. A boy's voice: "ചേച്ചീ… എന്റെ മുറിയിലെ ക്ലോക്ക് നാൽപത് അടിച്ചു."
- 1:10 Meera looks at her wrist. The tick has the same rhythm as the boy's breathing.
**Count card:** 14.

## Ep 3 · 13 · ക്ലോക്കുകളുടെ നഗരം (City of Clocks)
**Hook:** A long corridor of an apartment block; every neighbour standing in their doorway, staring, perfectly still.
**Camera path:** Aadi's door → the long corridor → past each doorway → the stairwell → back to Aadi's door.
- 0:08 3:00 AM. Aadi opens his door for water.
- 0:15 Every door on the floor is open. Every neighbour stands in it, facing him.
- 0:30 The camera tracks slowly past them. Their eyes don't move. Their chests don't move. Only the ticking.
- 0:50 Mrs. Thomas from 4B whispers without moving her lips: "മുപ്പത്തൊമ്പത്."
- 1:05 Aadi runs back. The camera circles round to his own doorway: **his mother** is standing in it, staring.
**Count card:** 13.

## Ep 4 · 12 · വൈകുന്ന പ്രതിബിംബം (Late Reflection)
**Hook:** Arjun's reflection smiling while Arjun himself is not.
**Camera path:** Arjun's barber-shop mirror job at night → into the mirror → the reflected shop → back out on the other side.
- 0:08 Arjun works late, sweeping. Mirrors on three walls.
- 0:20 His reflection is always half a second late. He has learned to ignore it.
- 0:40 He stops. The reflection keeps sweeping.
- 0:55 The camera passes *into* the mirror. The reflection turns and speaks to us. **പ്രതിബിംബം:** "നിന്നെ തിരിച്ചയച്ചത് ഞാനാ. ഇനി ഞാൻ തിരിച്ചു പോകണം."
- 1:10 It writes on the fogged glass from the inside, backwards: ആദി.
**Count card:** 12.

## Ep 5 · 11 · മായ്ച്ചുകളഞ്ഞ മകൾ (The Erased Daughter)
**Hook:** A burnt 1946 photograph: Elias the clockmaker and a little girl whose face has been scratched out.
**Camera path:** Ammachi's veranda in the rain → into the photograph → 1946 in sepia → back out on the same veranda.
- 0:08 Meera visits Ammachi. **ആമ്മച്ചി:** "1946-ൽ അവർ ഒരാളെയല്ല ഗോപുരത്തിൽ പൂട്ടിയത്."
- 0:25 The camera sinks into the photo. Sepia village. Elias dragged away. A little girl hidden in the tower, winding the clock to stay brave.
- 0:50 The town bricks up the door. She keeps winding. She sings the lullaby, the one Meera knows.
- 1:05 Back on the veranda, the rocking chair rocks by itself. **ആമ്മച്ചി:** "അവൾ ഇപ്പോഴും എണ്ണുകയാ, മോളേ."
**Count card:** 11.

## Ep 6 · 10 · നോക്കിനിൽക്കുന്നവർ (Watchers)
**Hook:** A crowded street at night, everyone frozen, all with clock faces.
**Camera path:** a single continuous walk with Aadi down a market street.
- 0:08 Aadi walks home late. The street goes quiet.
- 0:20 People stand still. Their faces slowly become clock faces.
- 0:35 Rule: they move only when you blink. The screen dips to black for a blink; they are closer.
- 0:55 Another blink, closer. Aadi stops blinking; tears run.
- 1:10 He blinks. A Watcher is right in front of him, its hands pointing straight at him.
**Count card:** 10.

## Ep 7 · 9 · നേഹയുടെ മുന്നറിയിപ്പ് (Neha's Warning)
**Hook:** Neha, soaked, standing at the foot of Meera's bed, water pooling.
**Camera path:** Meera's ceiling → down to the bed → follow the water trail to the bathroom → into the bathtub's dark water → back up.
- 0:08 Drip… drip… Meera wakes. A trail of water across the floor.
- 0:20 Neha (from Season 1), dripping, glitching like VHS. **നേഹ:** "നീ അവനെ തോൽപ്പിച്ചില്ല. നീ അവനെ ഊട്ടുകയായിരുന്നു."
- 0:40 **മീര:** "ഞാൻ ഘടികാരം പിന്നോട്ട് തിരിച്ചല്ലോ…"
- 0:50 **നേഹ:** "ഓരോ തിരിക്കലും അവൾക്ക് ഒരു വർഷം കൂടി കൊടുത്തു."
- 1:05 Neha sinks into the bathtub water. From the water, a small hand reaches up and grips the edge.
**Count card:** 9.

## Ep 8 · 8 · രണ്ടാമത്തെ ഗോപുരം (The Second Tower)
**Hook:** Under the city, a buried clock tower upside down, its bell hanging up into the dark.
**Camera path:** a metro tunnel → down a maintenance shaft → into the buried tower → up its inverted stairs.
- 0:08 Meera and Aadi follow the ticking into the closed metro line.
- 0:25 A crack in the tunnel wall. Behind it, stone, and an old clock face.
- 0:45 A second tower, buried upside down beneath the city.
- 1:00 Its clock runs backwards. Its bell is still swinging.
- 1:10 **ആദി:** "ചേച്ചീ… അത് നമ്മുടെ പേരാ." Their names are carved around the clock face, among dozens of others.
**Count card:** 8.

## Ep 9 · 7 · തിരിക്കരുത് (Don't Wind It)
**Hook:** Meera's hand on the winding key, trembling.
**Camera path:** around the buried tower's gear room in one slow circle.
- 0:08 Meera finds the key, the same key from Season 1.
- 0:20 Instinct: wind it back, like last time.
- 0:30 Every turn, a scream somewhere in the city above (distant, layered).
- 0:45 Arjun's voice from the dark: "നിർത്ത്, മീരാ! ഓരോ വട്ടവും ഒരാളെ കൊണ്ടുപോകുന്നു!"
- 1:00 She stops. Silence. Then the key keeps turning **by itself**.
- 1:12 A girl's small hand on the other side of the gears, winding.
**Count card:** 7.

## Ep 10 · 6 · ആമ്മച്ചി (Ammachi)
**Hook:** Ammachi's empty rocking chair, still warm, still rocking.
**Camera path:** Ammachi's house from attic to veranda, following her.
- 0:08 Ammachi climbs to the attic with the old pocket watch.
- 0:25 **ആമ്മച്ചി:** "1986-ൽ ഞാൻ ഒളിച്ചു. ഇത്തവണ ഞാൻ പോകും, അവർക്കു പകരം."
- 0:45 She winds her own pocket watch forward and calls into the dark: "എന്നെ എടുത്തോ."
- 1:00 Every clock in the house stops. The chair rocks once. She is gone.
- 1:10 On the floor, her watch, now ticking with a child's heartbeat.
**Count card:** 6.

## Ep 11 · 5 · അർജുന്റെ ഉള്ളിൽ (Inside Arjun)
**Hook:** Arjun's open shirt: clockwork where his ribs should be.
**Camera path:** into Arjun's chest through the gears and out through his eyes.
- 0:08 Arjun can barely stand. Under his skin, gears grind.
- 0:25 **അർജുൻ:** "മീരാ… എന്റെ ഹൃദയം ഇപ്പോ അവളുടെ ഘടികാരമാ."
- 0:45 The camera travels inside him: gears, springs, a tiny pendulum swinging where his heart was.
- 1:00 **അർജുൻ:** "എന്നെ നിർത്ത്. അവൾ എന്നിലൂടെ കേൾക്കുന്നു."
- 1:12 His eyes turn into tiny clock faces. Both hands point at Meera.
**Count card:** 5.

## Ep 12 · 4 · അവളുടെ പാട്ട് (The Daughter's Song)
**Hook:** A music box opening by itself in a dark room; a tiny dancer with a clock face turns.
**Camera path:** the music box → into its mechanism → a memory of the girl singing → back out into Meera's room.
- 0:08 Meera hums the Season 1 lullaby to calm Aadi.
- 0:20 Somewhere, a music box joins in, half a beat behind.
- 0:35 The melody plays backwards. The words change.
- 0:50 **അവൾ (singing):** "താരാട്ടല്ല ഇത്… വിളിയാണ്…"
- 1:05 Meera realises: every time anyone sang it, they called her closer.
- 1:15 Aadi, eyes closed, still singing it in his sleep.
**Count card:** 4.

## Ep 13 · 3 · എല്ലാ ഘടികാരങ്ങളും നിലയ്ക്കുന്നു (Every Clock Stops)
**Hook:** A bird frozen mid-flight in the air; rain stopped in mid-fall.
**Camera path:** the longest glide: through the frozen city, between motionless raindrops, past frozen people.
- 0:08 3:00 AM. The whole city stops. Rain hangs in the air.
- 0:20 Only Meera and Aadi can move.
- 0:35 They walk through frozen crowds. Every face is turned toward the old tower.
- 0:55 The raindrops slowly start to fall **upwards**.
- 1:10 From the tower, a girl's voice counts: "മൂന്ന്…"
**Count card:** 3.

## Ep 14 · 2 · അവളുടെ മുഖം (Her Face)
**Hook:** The girl's hair parting, slowly; then cut to black just before we see.
**Camera path:** up the original tower's spiral stairs to the clock room, one continuous climb.
- 0:08 Meera and Aadi climb. The stairs repeat, the same crack, the same window.
- 0:30 At the top, the girl sits winding the great clock, her back to them.
- 0:45 **മീര:** "നിന്റെ പേരെന്താ?" **അവൾ:** "ആരും ചോദിച്ചിട്ടില്ല."
- 1:00 She turns. **Her face is shown:** a little girl's face, half porcelain, half clockwork, crying.
- 1:12 **അവൾ:** "എന്നെ ഓർക്കാൻ ആരുമില്ലെങ്കിൽ… എല്ലാവരും എന്നോടൊപ്പം നിൽക്കണം."
**Count card:** 2.

## Ep 15 · 1 · പൂജ്യം (Zero)
**Hook:** The great clock's hands meeting at zero.
**Camera path:** a slow circle around the clock room that ends looking out over the waking city at dawn.
- 0:08 The last chime begins. Everything shakes.
- 0:20 Meera understands. She doesn't wind the clock. She kneels and asks the girl her name.
- 0:35 **അവൾ (whisper):** "ഏലിയാസിന്റെ മകൾ… ക്ലാര."
- 0:45 **മീര:** "ക്ലാരാ. ഇനി ഞങ്ങൾ ഓർക്കും." Meera scratches **ക്ലാര** into the clock face with the key.
- 1:00 The clock stops forever. The gear under Meera's wrist goes still. Clara fades into light.
- 1:10 Dawn. Every clock in the city starts again at 3:01. Aadi laughs.
- 1:18 Final shot: in Meera's mirror, her reflection blinks… half a second late.
**Count card:** 0.
