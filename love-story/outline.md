# ഒരു റിപ്ലൈ (One Reply) — love story outline

**Heroine renamed Nila → Manjusha (27 Nov).**

**Status:** saved for after COUNTDOWN Season 2. Malayalam first (Hindi/Tamil later if it works).
**Based on:** a real experience of the series creator. **All characters are fictional**: the woman's name, job, city and account are invented, so no real person can be recognised.

## Characters
| Character | Who |
|---|---|
| **Aadhi (22)** | Quiet engineering graduate. Scrolls Instagram alone every night at 2 AM. |
| **Manjusha (25)** | Dance creator with 500K followers. Warm on camera, tired off it. 3 years older than Aadhi. |
| **Collab page admin** | Runs a page Manjusha collaborated with; passes on Aadhi's message. |
| **Manjusha's mother** | Receives the parcel; the family's fear drives the turning point. |
| **Aadhi's friend** | Brings the news that Manjusha's family is thinking of a police case. |

## Episode beats
1. **2:14 AM.** Aadhi stops on one reel and watches it on loop.
2. **The wall.** 500K followers, DMs closed. He knows thousands feel the same.
3. **The collab page.** He finds a page she collaborated with and sends a message through them, expecting nothing.
4. **One reply.** "Thank you so much for the support 🤍" He reads it a hundred times. (The happiest episode.)
5. **Getting closer.** He learns she's 3 years older and decides it doesn't matter. He pauses a video and spots a house number in the background.
6. **The gift.** He sends a birthday gift and a handwritten letter. Shown from **her side**: her mother asks who sent a parcel to their home; Manjusha goes quiet and scared.
7. **The warning.** Through a friend of a friend he hears her family is talking about a police case. He finally sees it the way she did.
8. **The letter he never sends.** He writes everything he felt, then deletes it. He stops completely.
9. **What he does with the love.** He starts making his own reels and stories and puts everything he felt into his work.
10. **Ending A (chosen): bittersweet.** Months later, a notification: *"Manjusha liked your reel."* He smiles, puts the phone down without replying, and looks out at the morning.
    Final title: **"ചില ഇഷ്ടങ്ങൾ… ഒരു റിപ്ലൈയിൽ പൂർണമാണ്."** ("Some loves are complete with just one reply.")

## Tone and message
- Relatable late-night longing, the joy of one reply, then heartbreak and growing up.
- Finding her address and sending gifts is shown as the **mistake** that scared her, not as romance. Stepping back is the real act of love.
- Ending A leaves a small door open for a possible Part 2.

## Format (decided)
- **10 episodes × 1:30**, in **Malayalam, Hindi and Tamil** (Hindi/Tamil with English subtitles).
- **Feel-good romantic title song** in all 3 languages (full song, promo cut, lyric video, like COUNTDOWN).
- **Style B, hybrid:** about **5 key moments per episode are real animated video** (AI image-to-video from the storyboard panels); the rest are cinematic panels with camera moves, warm night-time colours and phone-screen UI.
  - Key moments, for example: the 2:14 AM reel, the reply notification, the parcel being opened, the deleted letter, the final "liked your reel".
  - Animated clips carry no dialogue, so one set serves all three languages. Voices come as thoughts, voice notes and phone moments to avoid lip-sync problems.

## Music and "no-skip" plan (26 Nov)
**Full romantic background score under every second**, never silent or dry.
- **One main theme**, taken from the title song "ഒരു റിപ്ലൈ" melody, so the series sounds like one film. It comes back in different moods:
  | Cue | Mood | Used in |
  |---|---|---|
  | 2 AM Alone | soft piano + rain, lonely | Ep1–2, late-night scrolling |
  | Hope | guitar + light strings | Ep3, sending the message |
  | **The Reply** | full theme, warm strings, joyful | Ep4 notification, happy montage (song chorus) |
  | Falling | piano + cello, sweet but uneasy | Ep5–6 |
  | Fear (her side) | low strings, heartbeat | Ep6–7 parcel, police case |
  | Letting Go | solo piano, slow | Ep8 deleted letter |
  | New Dawn | theme returns, bright, hopeful | Ep9–10, ending |
- **Sound design:** phone notification pings, typing sounds, rain, night city, heartbeat at key moments, so it feels real.
- Credits: score cues ~100–200 (generated music) or free (built from the song's own stems/melody).

**Retention rules (so people don't skip):**
1. **First 2 seconds = strongest moment** of the episode (the notification, the parcel, the "liked your reel"), then the story.
2. Every episode **ends on a cliffhanger** + the theme swelling.
3. **No dead air**: music + sound always playing, scenes change every 2–4 s.
4. Phone-screen moments (DMs, notification, "seen") as the visual hook, because everyone has felt them.
5. Each episode ~1:30 max, with a recap feel only through visuals, not text overlays.

## Music decision (26 Nov): trial Ep1 version 9 approved ✅
Approved file: `trial/out/ONE_REPLY_trial_ep01_ml_9x16_v9.mp4` (score `trial/audio/score_v9.mp3`, plan `trial/audio/score_plan_v9.json`).
**Series sound recipe** (from analysing "Muthuchippi", Thattathin Marayathu, and 90s Malayalam romantic melodies):
- Modern Malayalam romantic acoustic background score, ~84 BPM, 4/4, warm major key (I–V–vi–IV).
- **Soft wordless humming throughout**: female humming opens, male humming joins in harmony on the big romantic moments.
- **Bansuri flute leads the love melody**; long flute solo for admiring moments.
- Warm acoustic guitar fingerpicking, soft finger snaps, shaker, warm bass; glockenspiel sparkle; strings swell only at the peaks.
- Music stops on the cliffhanger beat; humming + flute return on the title card.
- Mix: score at -2.5 dB under the voice (ducked 5 dB during lines), final -13 to -14 LUFS.
- What did NOT work (v1–v8): sad/dark piano, western film-score strings, "feel-good" without humming, using the title song with vocals under the scenes.
