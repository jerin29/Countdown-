# How to schedule Hindi & Tamil in Meta Business Suite

Folders: `hindi/` (26 posts, 9:20 PM IST) and `tamil/` (26 posts, 9:40 PM IST).
Each post = one `.mp4` + one `.txt` with the same number. `SCHEDULE.md` in each folder lists every date.

For each post:
1. Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com or the app) → **Create reel**.
2. Choose the right Instagram account (@kili_poyi_hindi or the Tamil account) – untick Facebook if you don't want it there.
3. Upload the `.mp4`.
4. Open the matching `.txt`, copy everything, paste as the caption.
5. **Schedule** → date from the file name, time 9:20 PM (Hindi) / 9:40 PM (Tamil).
6. Turn on the **AI info label** if Meta asks (the series is made with AI).

Tip: Meta lets you schedule up to about 29 days ahead, so everything up to 25 Dec can be scheduled now.

After you've scheduled them, tell Claude so it can cancel the same posts on @kili_poyi_ai in vidIQ (to avoid double posting).
