## Meaning (English)
- **Intro:** A lullaby hum ("aariraro"), then the clock ticking.
- **Verse 1 (episodes 1–4):** Ten chimes in the high tower; nine for those who went into the dark; eight for the girl with the blue ribbons (Neha); seven for the friend who never knew.
- **Chorus:** *Don't count, don't count, even if you hear the bells. Don't turn around, don't turn around, even if a shadow comes behind you. At three o'clock he comes, slowly through the mist. Sing "aariraro" and sleep... he is listening, slowly.*
- **Verse 2 (episodes 5–8):** Six by the foggy roadside; five in the clock with a name; four for the hours you'll never find; three for the empty chair that rocks.
- **Bridge (the legend):** *Long ago, a clockmaker... locked away for a crime he never did... he died at three in the morning... "Every moment you took... I will take back."*
- **Verse 3 (finale):** Two little hearts in the brass gears; one... at the last chime. *Wind it backward, slowly wind it back; time will return, the darkness will fade.*
- **Final chorus:** *Don't count, even when the chimes are over. Sleep, sleep, even when dawn comes. When forty years pass, the tower will wake... aariraro... and then he'll look for you again.*
- **Outro:** *Close your eyes and sleep, my darling... at three o'clock he's coming... tonight.*
