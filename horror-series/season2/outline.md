## Logline
Season 1 ended with a boy waking at 3 AM in a city apartment and a voice whispering **"Forty."**
This time the clock doesn't chime ten times. It counts down from **forty**, and Meera's winding the clock back didn't end the curse: it **moved** it. **Fifteen nights.** Every night the Keeper takes **more than one**. And this time, the taken don't come back.

## What's darker in Season 2
- **No reset button.** The people who are taken stay gone. Season 1's "wind it back" trick is now the trap.
- **The Keeper changes.** His clock face now has **hands**, and they move. When both hands point at you, you have one night left.
- **The horror comes into the home.** Every clock, phone, microwave and wall clock in the city shows 3:00 when he's near.
- **Body horror (kept within Instagram/YouTube limits):** people slowly turning into clockwork, ticking under the skin, gear-shaped marks.
- **No safe adults.** The adults who remember start acting wrong.

## Dark title song (Malayalam · Hindi · Tamil)
- **Concept:** the Season 1 lullaby **reversed in meaning**. A slow, broken music box, a low male choir under a girl's voice, deep bells, ticking slowing down, and a final whispered count from forty.
- **Hook line idea:** "നാൽപത് മണിയൊച്ച... ഇനി ആരും ഉണരില്ല" (Forty chimes... and no one wakes again), with matching Hindi and Tamil lines.
- About 3 minutes per language, plus a promo cut, a lyric video, and end-card hums like Season 1.

## Sound design ("scary even without headphones")
- **Phone-speaker scares:** effects concentrated in the 1–5 kHz range that phone speakers reproduce well: metal scrapes, a child's breathing, bone-like ratchet clicks, distorted bells, a violin scream.
- **Headphone scares:** whispers that move from ear to ear, ticking that circles the listener, and a sub-bass drone under everything.
- **Dynamics:** 0.8–1.2 seconds of total silence before each jump scare, then a loud, sharp hit (peaks near −1 dBTP, overall −14 LUFS).
- **Signature sound:** the old bell slowed down, played backward, with a breath at the end.

## Visual upgrades (agreed after Season 1 results)
- **Hook-first openings:** every episode opens on its scariest moment in the first 2 seconds, then the headphone warning, then the story. No slow fog/title intro.
- **Style: one seamless long take per episode** (inspired by Cinemagic's single-take 2D science film). No hard cuts: the camera glides continuously through one big painted world, and each scene flows into the next through doors, mirrors, clock faces, darkness and match-moves. Layered parallax (foreground, characters, background), slow drifting camera, light and fog moving, characters' small motions (breathing, blinking, head turns, hands) done with the 2D rig. Built with our own tools, so no AI video credits (about 700 credits total for voices and songs).
- **Why:** Season 1 data showed the story and sound work (Ep 1: 10 shares, 8 saves per ~1,000 plays) but more than half of viewers swiped away in the first 2 seconds.

- **Real recorded sounds** instead of Season 1's code-synthesised effects where possible: real creaks, metal, breath, whispers, footsteps, clock mechanisms (licensed/royalty-free libraries), layered with the synthesised tones.
