# AGAIN (വീണ്ടും) — next series after ഒരു റിപ്ലൈ
Title chosen by the creator (27 Nov): **Again**. Theme: he falls, gets up and plays again. Heart of the story: the mirror line "ഇത് ഒന്നിന്റെയും അവസാനമല്ല, എല്ലാത്തിനും തുടക്കമാണ്."
Based on the creator's real life (told 27 Nov). Same anime style; hero = **Aadhi** (a prequel/sequel in the One Reply universe).

## Real events (creator's account)
- School in Kottayam: joins handball. In Plus Two, gets **junior state selection**; practice camps.
- The match is in Malappuram. There, his coach and another coach have a dispute, so **he isn't allowed to play** — he watches the others from the side, helpless.
- Back home, in front of the mirror: "ഇത് ഒന്നിന്റെയും അവസാനമല്ല, എല്ലാത്തിനും തുടക്കമാണ്. ഈ ചെറുത് പോയാലും കുഴപ്പമില്ല, എനിക്ക് വേണ്ടി വലിയ ലക്ഷ്യങ്ങൾ ഉണ്ട്."
- College: engineering (One Reply happens during these years). Joins every sport, wins nothing.
- Third year: a **new sir** joins and starts a **handball team**.
- Open selection at a college in Thiruvananthapuram → he makes the **district team**. At the next level he isn't selected — but he is happy: the state chance was taken from him once; now he reached district level by his own work.
- He leaves college having **founded a team that still exists**; new students keep joining it.
