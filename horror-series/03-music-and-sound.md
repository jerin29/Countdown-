# COUNTDOWN — Music, Songs & Sound Design

The whole score is built from **one idea**: a broken **music box** plus a **clock tick**. Every cue is a variation on that, so the series sounds like one world.

- **Key:** D minor (the finale resolves to D major)
- **Tempo:** 60 BPM, so **one beat = one tick = one second**. The clock literally is the metronome.
- **Core instruments:** music box, detuned celesta, prepared piano, low cello/double-bass drones, a single tubular bell (the chime), a whispering children's choir, reversed piano, and tape hiss.

---

## 1. Theme Song: "Ten Little Chimes" (the lullaby)

The series' signature song is a creepy counting nursery rhyme. Neha hums it, it plays on every end card, and the full version runs in the finale and the end credits.

**Melody idea (music box, D minor, 3/4 waltz, slow):**
```
D  F  A  |  G  F  E  |  D  -  -  |  A, -  -  |
Ten lit-tle | chimes in the | tow - er | high,
D  F  A  |  Bb A  G  |  A  -  -  |  -  -  -  |
nine for the | ones who | said good- | bye...
```
(Use this as a guide for a composer or AI music tool. Keep it simple, so a child could hum it.)

### Lyrics: "Ten Little Chimes"

> **Verse 1**
> Ten little chimes in the tower high,
> Nine for the ones who said goodbye,
> Eight for the girl with the ribbons blue,
> Seven for the friend who never knew.
>
> **Verse 2**
> Six on the road where the fog rolls in,
> Five little clocks with a name within,
> Four little hours you'll never find,
> Three for the chair that's left behind.
>
> **Bridge (whispered)**
> Don't count... don't count...
> He's listening, he's listening...
> Tick... tock... tick...
>
> **Final verse**
> Two little hearts in the gears of brass,
> One little chime... and then you're past.
> Hush now, child, close your eyes tight —
> **The Keeper's coming at three tonight.**

**Episode usage:** Each end card hums **only one line**: the line for that episode's number. By Episode 10, the audience has heard the whole song in pieces. The finale plays it in full, then **reversed**.

### Finale / Credits version: "Wind It Back" (hopeful reprise, D major)

> I counted every step in the dark,
> Every chime left a little mark,
> But hours aren't stolen, hours are given,
> Wind it back... and let them be living.
>
> Ten little chimes — now let them rest,
> Home is the place the lost love best,
> Open your eyes, the night is through...
> *(whisper)* ...but forty years will come for you.

## 3. Sound Effects (SFX) List

| SFX | Notes | Episodes |
|-----|-------|----------|
| **The Chime** | One deep, slightly out-of-tune bell (tubular bell or church bell, pitched down). **Use the exact same sound every time.** It is the brand. | All |
| **Tick** | A dry, close, old pocket-watch tick. | All |
| **Keeper footsteps** | Ticking instead of steps, with a slow metallic creak. | 3, 5, 6, 9, 10 |
| **Clunk** | A heavy gear engaging. | 1, 2 |
| **VHS glitch** | Tape warble plus static burst (for Neha). | 3, 9, 10 |
| **Wet drip** | Water dripping (Neha's presence). | 3 |
| **Rocking chair creak** | Wood creak, slow and regular. | 8 |
| **Gear grind** | Huge brass gears turning, metal-on-metal. | 9, 10 |
| **Reverse whoosh** | Reversed reverb tail (time rewinding). | 10 |
| **Phone UI** | Record beep, ringtone, message ping, glitch. | 5, 6, 7 |
| **Ambience** | Night crickets (which stop when the Keeper is near), wind, distant dogs, fog. | All |

**Free SFX sources:** Freesound.org (check each license), Pixabay Sound Effects, Mixkit, the YouTube Audio Library.

**Golden rule of horror sound:** Silence is the scariest sound. Cut **all** audio for about 0.5–1 second right before each big scare.

## 5. Mixing Guide (for 1:30 episodes on phone speakers)

- Dialogue: about −12 dB. Music bed: −22 to −18 dB under dialogue. The chime may peak at −3 dB.
- Final loudness: **−14 LUFS** (YouTube/Instagram standard).
- Low-end rumble doesn't play on phones. Add some mid-range "grit" (metal scrape, tape hiss) so tension still comes across.
- Keep the **End Card Hum** and **Chime** identical across all 10 episodes. Repetition builds the brand.
