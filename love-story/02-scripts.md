# ഒരു റിപ്ലൈ (One Reply) — Episode scripts (draft for approval)

10 episodes × ~1:25 · vertical 9:16 · anime style · Malayalam first (Hindi/Tamil voices later, same pictures).
Release: title song 31 Dec · Ep1 1 Jan · Ep2–Ep10 2–10 Jan, 9 PM.

**Rules for every episode**
- **0–2 s = strongest moment** of the episode (flash-forward), then the story.
- Always ends on a **cliffhanger** as the music swells or stops.
- Music: approved **v9 sound** (soft humming + bansuri flute + acoustic guitar, ~84 BPM). It stops dead on bad news.
- No text on screen except real phone screens (Instagram UI, notifications, DMs).
- Picture budget per episode: ~8 anime images (~100 vidIQ credits) + 1–2 animated clips (40 each).

**Cast:** Aadhi (22, quiet engineering graduate) · Manjusha (25, dancer, *manjusha.dances*, 500K) · Kiran (Aadhi's best friend, teasing but kind) · Manjusha's Amma · the admin of *kochi.dance.collective* (only seen as messages).
**Voices:** Aadhi = Akhil (inner voice). Kiran, Manjusha and Amma need new voices (choose when ElevenLabs is back).

## Production notes
- **Image count:** about 80 new anime images + 8–10 clips for Ep2–10 ≈ **1,350–1,800 vidIQ credits** (shared by all three languages).
- **Voices:** Aadhi (Akhil), plus three new voices (Kiran, Manjusha, Amma) to choose when ElevenLabs billing is fixed.
- **Privacy:** no real addresses, house names or phone numbers are ever readable on screen; *kochi.dance.collective* and *manjusha.dances* are fictional.
- **Hindi/Tamil:** same pictures and music; only the voice lines are translated (English meanings above).
