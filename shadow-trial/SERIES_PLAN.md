# THE SHADOW TRIAL (നിഴൽ വിചാരണ) — Mini-Series Production Plan

**Format:** 8 episodes × 1:30–2:00 min · 16:9 · Malayalam dialogue + English subtitles
**Look:** photoreal cinematic (live-action look, not cartoon) — techno-noir: rain, sodium/neon light,
deep blacks, teal-blue shadows, red accents from the live-stream screens. 2.39:1 letterbox, film grain.
**Violence rule:** implied, never gore on screen — shadows, sound, reaction close-ups, blood only as dark streaks.

---

## 1. Script fixes applied (vs. the 10-page master draft)

| Problem in draft | Fix used in this plan |
|---|---|
| Victim called "Rahul" and "Rohit" (Sc 7, 8, 12) | Always **Rahul Das** |
| Police inspector also named Rahul | Renamed **Inspector Arun** |
| "600 days ago" crash vs. fake death on Day 181–300 | **600 days = since Rahul's murder.** Highway crash happened **360 days ago** |
| Kidnap 2:15 AM, chamber "dawn", raid "night", Anjali says 3:45 AM | One night: **kidnap 11:40 PM → stream 12:30 AM → raid 3:45 AM → rooftop 5:00 AM dawn** |

## 2. Character lock (paste this description into every image/video prompt)

- **ANJALI / "SHADOW"** — Malayali woman, 32, after facial reconstruction: sharp high cheekbones, narrow
  jaw, dark intense eyes, shoulder-length black hair tied back, no makeup. As Shadow: black rain-coat,
  black hoodie, matte black half-mask, voice modulator. Old face (flashbacks only): softer round face,
  spectacles, long hair in a plait, lab coat.
- **RAHUL DAS** — 23, slim Malayali young man, short wavy hair, light stubble, white office shirt,
  lanyard ID "NEXUS TECH", carries a silver pendrive.
- **RONA MATHEWS** — 30s, tall, sleek straight hair with blonde highlights, red lipstick, black
  designer pantsuit, gold watch, high-heel boots. Arrogant, cold.
- **CHRISTY PHILIP** — 30s, short pixie-cut dyed burgundy hair, nose ring, leather jacket over black
  tee, nervous chain-smoker.
- **ACP VIKRAM NARAYAN IPS** — 45, broad-shouldered, salt-and-pepper short hair, thick moustache, khaki
  police uniform (on raid: black tactical vest over it).
- **INSPECTOR ARUN** — 30, lean, clean-shaven, glasses, khaki uniform.
- **ANAND (cyber expert)** — 28, hoodie, headphones around neck, multiple monitors.

**Key sets:** Walayar highway in rain · Velvet Lounge nightclub (purple/magenta neon) · basement
parking (flickering tube lights) · abandoned cold-storage factory with two glass chambers, steel
chairs, an 80-inch LED wall showing "THE PUBLIC TRIAL OF NEXUS DEVILS" · Cyber Police war room ·
Nexus Tower service lift · Infopark Sky Tower rooftop at dawn.

## 4. Production pipeline

1. **Character sheets** — 1 photoreal portrait per character (7 images) → reused as reference in every shot.
2. **Shot stills** — one photoreal still per shot (~100 total) using the character references.
3. **Motion** — image-to-video for each still (6–10 s), or 2.5D camera moves on stills for cheaper shots.
4. **Voices** — Malayalam dialogue per character (TTS or real voice actors — real actors sound far better).
5. **Edit** — assemble in HyperFrames: letterbox, grade, subtitles, sound design, music, title cards.
6. **Render** — 1080p MP4 per episode.
