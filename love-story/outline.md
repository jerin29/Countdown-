# ഒരു റിപ്ലൈ (One Reply) — love story outline

**Status:** saved for after COUNTDOWN Season 2. Malayalam first (Hindi/Tamil later if it works).
**Based on:** a real experience of the series creator. **All characters are fictional**: the woman's name, job, city and account are invented, so no real person can be recognised.

## Characters
| Character | Who |
|---|---|
| **Aadhi (22)** | Quiet engineering graduate. Scrolls Instagram alone every night at 2 AM. |
| **Nila (25)** | Dance creator with 500K followers. Warm on camera, tired off it. 3 years older than Aadhi. |
| **Collab page admin** | Runs a page Nila collaborated with; passes on Aadhi's message. |
| **Nila's mother** | Receives the parcel; the family's fear drives the turning point. |
| **Aadhi's friend** | Brings the news that Nila's family is thinking of a police case. |

## Episode beats
1. **2:14 AM.** Aadhi stops on one reel and watches it on loop.
2. **The wall.** 500K followers, DMs closed. He knows thousands feel the same.
3. **The collab page.** He finds a page she collaborated with and sends a message through them, expecting nothing.
4. **One reply.** "Thank you so much for the support 🤍" He reads it a hundred times. (The happiest episode.)
5. **Getting closer.** He learns she's 3 years older and decides it doesn't matter. He pauses a video and spots a house number in the background.
6. **The gift.** He sends a birthday gift and a handwritten letter. Shown from **her side**: her mother asks who sent a parcel to their home; Nila goes quiet and scared.
7. **The warning.** Through a friend of a friend he hears her family is talking about a police case. He finally sees it the way she did.
8. **The letter he never sends.** He writes everything he felt, then deletes it. He stops completely.
9. **What he does with the love.** He starts making his own reels and stories and puts everything he felt into his work.
10. **Ending A (chosen): bittersweet.** Months later, a notification: *"Nila liked your reel."* He smiles, puts the phone down without replying, and looks out at the morning.
    Final title: **"ചില ഇഷ്ടങ്ങൾ… ഒരു റിപ്ലൈയിൽ പൂർണമാണ്."** ("Some loves are complete with just one reply.")

## Tone and message
- Relatable late-night longing, the joy of one reply, then heartbreak and growing up.
- Finding her address and sending gifts is shown as the **mistake** that scared her, not as romance. Stepping back is the real act of love.
- Ending A leaves a small door open for a possible Part 2.

## Format (decided)
- **10 episodes × 1:30**, in **Malayalam, Hindi and Tamil** (Hindi/Tamil with English subtitles).
- **Feel-good romantic title song** in all 3 languages (full song, promo cut, lyric video, like COUNTDOWN).
- **Style B, hybrid:** about **5 key moments per episode are real animated video** (AI image-to-video from the storyboard panels); the rest are cinematic panels with camera moves, warm night-time colours and phone-screen UI.
  - Key moments, for example: the 2:14 AM reel, the reply notification, the parcel being opened, the deleted letter, the final "liked your reel".
  - Animated clips carry no dialogue, so one set serves all three languages. Voices come as thoughts, voice notes and phone moments to avoid lip-sync problems.
