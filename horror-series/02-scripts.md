# COUNTDOWN — Episode Scripts (10 × 1:30)

**Format key:** `TIME | VISUAL | AUDIO / DIALOGUE`
- **SFX** = sound effect. **BGM** = background music cue (see `03-music-and-sound.md`). **V.O.** = voice-over. **(O.S.)** = off-screen.
- The dialogue is deliberately short. At 1:30, silence and sound do most of the scaring.
- Each episode has about 120–160 spoken words, which leaves room for pauses.
